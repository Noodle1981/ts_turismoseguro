## Brand & Style

This design system expresses safety, sun-drenched hospitality, and verifiable authenticity. Built to eliminate rental fraud and mismatched expectations across San Juan's countryside properties (quintas, quinchos, and casas de campo), the visual language bridges high-trust institutional rigor with the warmth of outdoor celebration.

The style combines Modern Utility with Tactile Warmth:
- **Foundational Trust:** Deep mineral navy blue establishes institutional backing and certified physical inspections.
- **Sunlit Vibrancy:** Golden amber evokes the San Juan sun, paired with restorative emerald accents that highlight safety guarantees and inspected amenities.
- **Approachable Clarity:** Crisp white surfaces, soft framing contours, and generous spatial cadence ensure that families, event planners, and property owners move seamlessly from discovery to reservation without friction or skepticism.

## Colors

The palette operates on purposeful chromatic assignments that anchor credibility while highlighting outdoor leisure:

- **Primary (`#0F2942` - Deep Navy):** Represents verification authority, legal reassurance, and primary navigational weight. Used for header topography, primary interactive buttons, and structural grounding.
- **Secondary (`#059669` - Emerald Assurance):** Represents verified safety, physical audits, confirmed dates, and healthy outdoor foliage. Used for certification badges, confirmed status indicators, pool safety badges, and verified WhatsApp booking triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - San Juan Amber):** Represents daylight, warmth, premium highlights, and daytime shift selections (Día Completo / Sol). Used selectively for primary callouts, price accents, and active shift states.
- **Neutral & Canvas (`#0F172A`, `#64748B`, `#E2E8F0`, `#F8FAFC`, `#FFFFFF`):** Base canvas leverages pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated cards and warm porcelain slate (`#F8FAFC`) for page backdrops. Borders rely on `#E2E8F0` for gentle physical boundaries without aggressive contrast.

## Typography

The design system exclusively adopts **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to achieve cohesive geometric balance, exceptional numeral clarity (critical for rental rates, guest tiers, and phone numbers), and friendly humanistic curves.

- **Headlines:** Set in Bold (`700`) and ExtraBold (`800`) with tightened letter-spacing (`-0.02em`) to project confidence and editorial punch.
- **Body:** Set with generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.55`) to facilitate effortless reading of property rules, pool specs, and lease terms under bright outdoor sunlight.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Set in SemiBold (`600`) and Bold (`700`) with positive tracking for badges, verification dates, time stamps, and amenity categories.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes a 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop transitioning to a 4-column flow on mobile viewports:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and a max-width container of 1280px centered on screen. Property detail views split 7:5 between media/features and sticky reservation calculators.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with 20px gutters and 24px side margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 16px edge padding. Sticky quick-action bottom sheet houses the guest calculator and direct verified WhatsApp booking button.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Component interiors strictly adhere to 4px/8px multiples (`space-xs` through `space-xl`) to establish predictable visual cadence between amenities, tags, and pricing cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy prioritizes crisp clarity over heavy artificial drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` background without shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Container Surface):** Pure `#FFFFFF` resting on a 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border with a subtle warm tinted shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating & Hover):** Lifted property cards and dropdown selectors incorporate `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Booking Calculators & Verification Modals):** High-priority focus overlays employ `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.06)`.
- **Verification Seal Depth:** The 'Verificado In Situ' badge utilizes a subtle internal tint layer (`rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.08)`) with a crisp emerald border (`#059669`) rather than heavy drop shadow, denoting official legal attestation.

## Shapes

The roundedness strategy uses unified Level 2 radii (`0.5rem` / `8px` base) to reflect warm hospitality while avoiding excessive playfulness:

- **Standard Elements (8px / `0.5rem`):** Applied to form fields, shift segment controls, secondary buttons, and amenity tags.
- **Containers & Property Cards (16px / `1rem`):** Applied to listing cards, photo galleries, and quotation modules.
- **Floating Modals & Inspection Badges (24px / `1.5rem` to Pill):** High-touch verification pills and floating WhatsApp actions use fully rounded caps for tactile ergonomics on mobile touch screens.

## Components

### 1. In Situ Verification Badge
- **Structure:** Pill shape containing a micro shield-check icon, text label `"Verificado In Situ"`, and metadata `"Insp: [DD/MM/AAAA]"`.
- **Visuals:** Emerald tint background (`rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.08)`), border 1px solid `#059669`, text `#065F46` in `label-sm`. Overlay positioned on the top-left corner of property imagery with backdrop blur (`4px`).

### 2. Shift Selector (Selector de Turno)
- **Structure:** Segmented toggle card presenting two primary day options:
  - **Día Completo:** `10:00 a 20:00 hs` (Sun icon, amber active accent `#F59E0B`).
  - **Tarde / Noche:** `18:00 a 02:00 hs` (Moon/Dusk icon, navy active accent `#0F2942`).
- **Interactive States:** Selected state features a 2px outer border in primary tone with a subtle 5% background wash; unselected has 1px `#E2E8F0`.

### 3. Daytime Amenity Chips
- **Chips:** `Pileta cercada`, `Parrilla techada`, `Freezer bebidas`, `Parque arbolado`, `Vajilla completa`, `Sonido moderado`.
- **Style:** Compact pill layout (`rounded-full`), border 1px `#E2E8F0`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#334155` (`label-md`).
- **Safety Critical:** `Pileta cercada` carries a dedicated emerald check prefix icon to reassure families with small children.

### 4. Interactive Guest Quotation Calculator
- **Structure:** Stepper component (+ / - controls) for headcounts (e.g., 20, 40, 60+ personas) with dynamic per-tier price feedback.
- **Conversion CTA:** Large primary button in Emerald Green (`#059669`) featuring the official WhatsApp monogram, labeled `"Reservar vía WhatsApp Verificado"`. Clicking pre-populates a verified message containing property ID, chosen shift, and guest volume.

### 5. Buttons & Inputs
- **Primary Button:** `#0F2942` solid background, `#FFFFFF` text, 8px border radius, hover state shifts to `#1E3A5F`.
- **Verified Action Button:** `#059669` solid background, hover state `#047857`.
- **Input Fields:** `#FFFFFF` background with 1px `#CBD5E1` border, transitioning to 2px `#0F2942` focus ring. Helper text in `#64748B`.
